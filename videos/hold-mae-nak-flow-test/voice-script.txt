Mae Nak is Thailand's most famous ghost.
While her husband Mak was away at war, Nak died giving birth to their child.
But when Mak came home, she was waiting for him, as if nothing had happened.
One night, as she prepared betel leaves, a lime rolled off the floor and fell under the house.
Mak watched her arm stretch, longer and longer, down through the floorboards, to pick it up.
That was the moment he knew.
His wife was a ghost.
Would you have run?

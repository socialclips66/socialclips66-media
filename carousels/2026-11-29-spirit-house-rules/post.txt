5 rules of the tiny house outside every Thai home 🏠 It's called a spirit house, and number 5 is why you see hundreds of them piled up in one place. Is there one near your home? Yes or no? 👇
Save this for your Thailand trip 📌 and send it to someone who has walked past one.

Fact check 🔎 FACT: a spirit house (san phra phum) is a small shrine for the guardian spirit of the land. A Brahmin priest or ritual expert usually picks its date, spot and direction, and red strawberry soda is one of the most common offerings. BELIEF: the main house's shadow must not fall on it, and an old one is never thrown in the bin; retired spirit houses gather at resting places, often beside temples. Nobody agrees on why the soda is red.
